A Vision for the Future of Global Health

The AHEAD (Applied Health Economics and Development) 2025 International Conference serves as a premier interdisciplinary platform for researchers, academicians, practitioners, and policymakers to exchange vital knowledge regarding the evolving landscape of global healthcare.

Our mission is to foster a collaborative environment where theoretical research meets practical application. By addressing critical challenges such as healthcare financing, climate-induced health crises, and digital health transformations, we aim to accelerate the creation of robust, equitable, and sustainable health systems worldwide.

Conference Theme

Global Disruptions in Healthcare and Development:
Challenges, Innovations, and Pathways for the 21st Century

The 21st century has brought unprecedented disruptions, from pandemics to climate change. Our theme centers on identifying these critical intersections and pioneering innovative pathways that integrate technological advancements with human-centric policy design.

Organizing Institutions

IIT Roorkee & AHEAD Lab

The Indian Institute of Technology Roorkee (IITR) is among the foremost institutes of national importance in higher technological education and engineering, basic and applied research in India. With a legacy spanning over 177 years, IIT Roorkee continues to spearhead innovation.

The AHEAD (Applied Health Economics and Development) Lab, based in the Department of Humanities and Social Sciences at IIT Roorkee, focuses on generating impactful research at the intersection of public policy, healthcare economics, and sustainable societal development.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions regarding participation and submission.

Who can participate in AHEAD 2025?

AHEAD 2025 is open to researchers, academicians, PhD scholars, policymakers, industry professionals, and students interested in health economics, sustainable development, public policy, and related fields.

How do I submit a paper?

You can submit your extended abstract or full paper through our online Conference Management Portal. Please visit the "Submission Guidelines" page for detailed formatting instructions and the submission link.

Will certificates be provided?

Yes, all registered participants and presenting authors will receive an official digital and physical certificate of participation/presentation from IIT Roorkee at the conclusion of the event.

Is online participation available?

Yes, AHEAD 2025 is organized as a hybrid conference. International delegates and participants facing travel constraints can choose the online registration tier to present their papers and attend sessions virtually.

What publication opportunities are available?

Selected high-quality full papers will be considered for publication in Scopus/WOS/ABDC indexed journals and edited volumes by reputed international publishers, subject to standard peer-review processes.

How can I contact the organizers?

You can reach the organizing committee via the "Contact" page, or email us directly at ahead@iitr.ac.in. We aim to respond to all queries within 24-48 hours.
